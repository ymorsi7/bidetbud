# BidetBud 60s demo - three storylines

**One thing:** BidetBud is the map of places that actually have a bidet - search, filter, verify, go.

**One thing to remember:** "I can find a real bidet near me, not a guess."

**Spine task:** Someone needs a bidet, opens BidetBud, filters and searches (ICSD), opens a verified spot, then sees Near me - 135 places within 10 miles.

---

## A (RECOMMENDED) - Spray into the map

**Device:** Type carries the argument, then the feature as the hook. **Form:** Object-to-object chain (word → world → window → phone → pin).

Viewer feels a wry cold open ("the West still hasn't figured out… Bidets."), then watches the planet light up with real pins and rides the live product through filter → search → detail → near me. Proof ends with real Astoria verification footage and the suggest form.

**First 2 s:** Big two-line cold open on a zinc ground; frame 0 is readable type, never a logo.

**Beats:** hook (cold open + Bidets.) → reveal (world pins + BidetBud) → proof (live UI verbs) → trust legend → phone + Moka clip → add a spot → end card

**Look:** Zinc `#09090b` ground, Inter, teal accent only on one word / pin / CTA per beat; real product screenshots; dot-matrix world from sealed pin cells.

**Sound:** Voiceover only (no music). Dry founder walkthrough timed to each beat.

---

## B - The afternoon that fails

**Device:** A moment in someone's day. **Form:** Match cuts (toilet door → map pin → phone directions).

A timestamped afternoon: "Need a bidet. Now." Wrong guesses and a bidet-less stall, then BidetBud Near me lands the verified spot and directions. Quieter, more personal, less product tour.

**First 2 s:** Phone lock screen + a text bubble "where's a bidet near me".

**Beats:** hook (need) → tension (wrong places) → reveal (map) → proof (near me + detail) → payoff (directions) → claim → end card

**Look:** Warm daylight city B-roll + product UI as relief; less kinetic type.

**Sound:** Sparse piano bed that blooms when the pin opens; city ambience under tension only.

---

## C - Endless zoom through one pin

**Device:** The result first. **Form:** Endless zoom.

Open on the Moka verification clip full-frame, pull back through the phone, into the map, out to the world of 10k+ pins, then crash into the end URL. Spectacle-first; less teaching of filters/search.

**First 2 s:** Handheld sprayer footage already playing.

**Beats:** payoff-first → pullback proof → world scale → claim → end card

**Look:** Live footage and product chrome only; almost no title cards.

**Sound:** Riser into impact at the pullback, then a bright bed through the world reveal.

---

**Pick:** A - Spray into the map. Best teaches what BidetBud does in 60 s with real UI and real evidence, which is what a stranger needs after "what it does."
