Nov 22, 2026 · Chicago, IL

Bridgeview Update

Bridgeview, Illinois, a town in the Chicagoland area that I have frequented on multiple occasions. A locality in which I would expect the bidets to be more than prominent. Alas, this is not the case.

Due to the fact that I tend to spend most of the time of Bridgeview visits in 6-7 different establishments, I will not claim that I have traversed its vast lands, as I have in some other regions. However, I will say that after visiting this lovely community multiple times, most recently today, I have been overcome with disappointment.

Jazwah Coffee, my favorite coffee/drink shop in all of Illinois, seems like the type of place to have the sense and expectation to install a bidet. Unfortunately, as I discovered last week, this is not the case. I have also ran my investigation through the latrine in M'Dakhan (my favorite restaurant in Illinois), across the street, which to my dismay, also lacked a bidet.

However, this case has brought me finally to ZamZam Sweets. I am proud to convey that they did in fact have a bidet, in addition to the best sweets, once again, in all of Illinois.

I must also bestow an honorable mention to Al-Manakeesh, which not only has the best manakeesh that I have had in Illinois, but also has an installed classic hose bidet.

My travels in previous years have brought me to a few other corners of this magnificent town of delicacies; alas, I cannot remember which contain the hose of power, and which do not.

I intend to update my investigation log during my future travels in the Greater Chicagoland Area, and approach the businesses that I have logged as bidet-less with the prospect of installing a 60 PSI water gun.

Do better, Bridgeview, and possibly one day with the help of BidetBud.

Nov 21, 2026 · New York, NY

From 14th Street heartbreak to a Steinway Street win

Was a cold morning in New York, one on which I decided to take a lengthy stroll in Manhattan while enjoying a cup of the Big Apple's famous joe. When the clock rang, I needed to find a local bidet. To my surprise while checking BidetBud, I found that there was a location sourced from the internet conveniently ten minutes away from me at 14th Street at a Five Guys. I decided to march my way over to this beacon of hope, and was guilted into buying a soda I didn't want so that I could ask to use their facilities. As I subsequently pop open the door to their WC, to my dismay I had discovered that the latrine was bidet-less.

I carried on my day with enough heartache and disappointment to kill an elderly man.

On my way back to my temporary abode in Queens Astoria, I spot a cafe, one reminding me of the ones I frequent in Egypt, that resparked in me a shimmer of hope; one that had elevated my mood in a way that only caffeine could rival.

Perhaps it was the child in me, or perhaps it was pure mindlessness, but I marched in that establishment with a feeling that I would at long last find what I was looking for. Once again, despite hitting the FDA's recommended maximum of caffeine intake, I purchased a drink with the sole intention of verifying whether the lavatory would prove my suspicions correct.

To my absolute delight, I found a bidet. Such an occasion deserved documentation; which I have left below to share with the world.

Thank you, Moka & Co of Queens Astoria on Steinway, may your bidet touch the rears of many, the way it touched my heart today.
