Quick answers

Which one should I buy if I only buy one?

Home: NEO 185. Small business / masjid: Brondell handheld. Upgrade to a TOTO seat only if you have an outlet and you are outfitting your own bathroom.

Do I need a plumber?

Usually no. These kits include a T-adapter that goes on the existing fill-valve hose. Shut the water off, swap the connector, turn it back on, check for drips. If your toilet is a one-piece skirted model, read the listing’s fit notes first.

Cold water or heated?

Non-electric units use whatever is in the tank line — typically cool. Fine for most people. Warm water means an electric seat (C5 / S5) or a model that taps the sink’s hot line.
