BidetBud — App Review notes
Build: 1.0.1 (7)  |  Bundle: com.bidetbud.app  |  Apple ID: 6815273990

1) How to review (no login)
There is no account registration, login, or account deletion.
There is no paid content and no In-App Purchases.

- Install and open. The map loads from bundled data; if online it may refresh from https://bidetbud.com/bidet-seed.json
- Search a city or place name, or tap Near me and allow location (used only to find nearby pins).
- Switch Map / List. Tap a pin or a list card. A short card slides up from the bottom with the name, bidet status, address, and Directions. Drag the handle up for source/details, or drag down / tap × to close.
- Filters: type (masajid, restaurants, hotels, public), country, guests-only, no-bidet.
- Suggest a spot: + button. Name + whether it has a bidet is enough. Optional photo uses the system photo picker (one image). We review before publish.
- Report: on a place card, Report → choose a reason → Send report.
No demo account. No sample files.

2) User-generated content
Anyone can suggest a spot or an update. Submissions are emailed to us via Web3Forms and are reviewed by a human before anything is published on the map. Users can report a wrong pin from the place card. There are no user profiles, so there is no “block user” control; the remedy is reporting the listing.

3) Purpose and audience
BidetBud is a free map of restrooms that have a bidet, handheld sprayer (shattaf), or washlet. Target audience: travelers and anyone who wants a washlet-style restroom, including people who use masajid, restaurants, hotels, and public facilities.

4) External services (core)
- OpenStreetMap / Carto tile servers — map tiles
- https://bidetbud.com/bidet-seed.json — public listing data (also bundled offline)
- Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com) — email for suggest-a-spot and reports (no user accounts)
- Device location via iOS (Near me only)
- GoatCounter — anonymous page-view counts (no ads, no tracking SDK)
No authentication provider, no payments, no AI API.

5) Regions
The app binary is the same in every region. Content is a worldwide map. Features do not change by storefront. Location permission is optional.

6) Regulated / third-party material
Not a regulated industry. Map tiles: OpenStreetMap / CARTO. Listings are compiled by BidetBud from user verification and cited public web/manufacturer sources.
